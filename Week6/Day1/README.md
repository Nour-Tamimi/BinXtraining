# Day 1 — Personal Notes

## What I Understood

A neuron is just a dot product plus a bias, passed through an activation function — nothing new
mathematically, just the Week 2 math applied in a new way. A neural network is many of these
stacked into layers, and "deep" just means more than one hidden layer. Training is the process of
adjusting the weights and bias until predictions improve.
